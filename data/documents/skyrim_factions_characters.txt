Skyrim features a large number of joinable factions, or guilds, each with its own questline and
set of notable characters. The Companions, based in Jorrvaskr in Whiterun, are a warrior guild
inspired by Ysgramor's original followers; its questline reveals that many senior members are
werewolves bound to Hircine through the "Blood of the Ancients" via their leader Kodlak
Whitemane, who ultimately seeks to be freed of the curse. The College of Winterhold is the
game's mage faction, led by Arch-Mage Savos Aren at the story's outset, and its questline
revolves around an ancient Eye of Magnus artifact and the villain Ancano.

The Thieves Guild, operating out of the Ragged Flagon beneath Riften, is run by Brynjolf and
guildmaster Mercer Frey, and its story involves the Nightingales, a group of agents secretly
sworn to the Daedric Prince Nocturnal in exchange for supernatural stealth abilities. The Dark
Brotherhood, an infamous guild of assassins, is contacted through a dark ritual known as the
Black Sacrament; its Skyrim chapter has been reduced to a small cell led by Astrid, operating
from a hidden sanctuary, and its questline touches on the Brotherhood's patron deity, the
Daedric Prince Sithis and the Night Mother.

The two political factions in Skyrim's civil war each have a clear leader: the Stormcloaks are
led by Ulfric Stormcloak, Jarl of Windhelm, who assassinated High King Torygg in single combat
(disputed as murder by using his Thu'um) and now leads the rebellion for Nord independence and
the right to worship Talos. The Imperial Legion side is commanded locally by General Tullius,
acting on behalf of the Empire and Emperor Titus Mede II.

Key non-faction characters include Delphine, a Blade (an ancient order of dragon-hunters loyal
to the Emperor's bloodline) who helps the Dragonborn investigate Alduin's return; Paarthurnax,
an elderly dragon living atop the Throat of the World who renounced Alduin's tyranny and now
mentors the Dragonborn in the Way of the Voice; and Esbern, the last remaining Blade scholar
with deep knowledge of dragon lore. Serana, introduced in the Dawnguard expansion, is an ancient
pureblood vampire who becomes a companion and central figure in that expansion's storyline.
